Precision-built switchgear, start to finish

Suraj Electricals specialises in manufacturing electrical switchboards — Power Control Centres, Motor Control Centres and Bus Ducts — from our 30,000 sq. ft. Gurugram facility, using advanced machinery including German-make CNC Shearing & Bending, CNC Turret Punch, CNC Busbar Cutting, i-Cut Legend Engraving Laser, MIG welding, an automatic bus bar sleeve heating machine and a 9-tank pre-treatment line with automatic conveyorised powder coating.
