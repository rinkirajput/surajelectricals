Founder · Est. 2017

Suraj Reswal

Founder, Suraj Electricals — Electrical Engineer

A graduate in Electrical Engineering, Suraj Reswal founded Suraj Electricals in 2017 as a complete electrical manufacturing company.

He started the company after gaining hands-on experience across the installation of power sector equipment, testing of electrical panels against drawings and schematics, and installation of electrical projects. Under his leadership, the company achieved rapid success with the help of a devoted, hardworking technical team.

Recognizing a growing market and clients in search of a result-oriented, techno-commercial partner, he built Suraj Electricals to complete every project-related job end to end.
