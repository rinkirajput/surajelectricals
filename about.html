Who We Are

Custom-built switchboards and control panels, engineered end to end.

Established in 2017, Suraj Electricals is a complete electrical manufacturing company focused on custom-built LT/HT switchboards, bus ducts, Power and Motor Control Centres, PLC and drive panels, and related products.

Our manufacturing and painting units operate from a modern production facility in Sector 76, Gurugram, Haryana — built across over 30,000 sq. ft. and equipped with German-make CNC shearing & bending, CNC turret punching, a 9-tank chemical pre-treatment line and automatic powder-coating conveyor, so every stage of fabrication and finishing happens in-house.

A skilled team of engineers, supervisors and support staff — backed by an in-house Design Office — lets us meet the specific requirements of different consultants with ease.
